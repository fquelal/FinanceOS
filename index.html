💼 FinanceOS
Your personal finance command center. Track income, bills, debt, and accounts in one place — with no subscriptions, no cloud sync, and no data leaving your device.
🔒 100% Private — Everything stays on your device
Getting Started
🏦
Add Your Accounts First
Add checking, savings, and cash accounts with real balances. Set a default account and it auto-selects in every payment modal. Overdraft warnings only work when balances are set.
"How do I track my bank balance?"
💰
Add Income Sources
Add salary, freelance, or any recurring income. Weekly and bi-weekly income automatically expands into individual dated cards each month. Tap any card to mark it Received or Missed.
"How does the app show my paychecks?"
📋
Add Bills & Subscriptions
Add recurring bills, credit cards, and loans. Each item gets a status (Pending, Scheduled, Paid, Overdue). The Monthly Health card at the top shows at a glance how the month looks.
"How do I track what I owe each month?"
🔁
Auto-Log Recurring Payments
Enable "Auto-log" on any bill or expense and FinanceOS logs the payment and debits your account automatically on the 1st of each month. No manual entry needed for recurring items.
"Can I stop manually logging the same things?"
Money Section
🥊
Monthly Health Card
At the top of Money you get a live health score for the month — Healthy, Caution, or At Risk. It shows overdue count, bills due this week, and net month in one glance.
"How is my month looking financially?"
📄
Tap-to-Expand Cards
Every item in the Money list starts collapsed showing just name, date, amount, and status. Tap any card to expand it and reveal actions — mark income Received, schedule a bill, edit, or delete.
"How do I mark a paycheck as received?"
📅
Filter by Type
Use the Type sort button to group everything by category: Income, Bills, Loans, and Credit Cards. Switch to Date view to see everything in chronological order across all types.
"How do I see just my income or just my bills?"
📆
Calendar View
Switch to Calendar view to see paychecks and bills plotted by date. Income chips are color-coded by status (Expected, Received, Missed). Bill chips are color-coded by due status.
"When are my bills and paychecks this month?"
Income & Paychecks
📅
Bi-Weekly Paycheck Calculator
Enter your last paycheck date and FinanceOS calculates exactly which months have 2 vs 3 paychecks. Each paycheck appears as its own card with the correct deposit date.
"Some months I get 3 paychecks — does the app know?"
🔢
Update Individual Payments
Each income occurrence can be updated independently. If one paycheck was short or had overtime, tap "$ Update" on that card to record the actual amount without changing the base schedule.
"My check was different this week — how do I update just that one?"
✅
Track Received vs Missed
Mark each paycheck as Received or Missed directly from the Money list. Status is stored per-occurrence — marking one paycheck received doesn't affect the others.
"How do I know which paychecks have actually cleared?"
Debt & Credit
📉
Debt Planner
Choose Avalanche (highest APR first) or Snowball (smallest balance first). See your exact payoff date and how much interest you'll save versus the other method.
"When will I be debt-free?"
📊
Loan Payoff Progress
Enter the original loan amount when adding a loan. The Debt Planner shows a progress bar with % paid off and amount remaining so you can see how far you have come.
"How much of my car loan have I paid off?"
🎁
Promo / 0% Offer Simulator
Got a 0% balance transfer offer or promotional APR? The simulator calculates the fee, monthly payment needed, and whether it's worth it before you commit.
"Is this balance transfer offer worth the fee?"
🌐
Go to Website & Confirm
Add a URL to any bill or debt. When scheduling a payment, a "Go to Website" button opens your bank or biller directly. Come back to the app and confirm the schedule when done.
"How do I jump to Chase to pay my credit card?"
Insights
📈
Cash Flow Chart
Insights → Cash Flow shows a monthly trend chart with Money In vs Money Out lines, followed by a detailed breakdown of every income source and outgoing item for the current month.
"Am I spending more than I earn month to month?"
👓
Spending Anomalies
The Spending tab automatically flags categories where you spent significantly more than your recent average. Great for catching lifestyle creep before it becomes a habit.
"Did I overspend somewhere this month?"
💹
APY Interest Tracking
Add an APY to any savings account. The account card shows your projected annual interest earned based on the current balance — updates live as your balance changes.
"How much interest will my HYSA earn this year?"
🏠
Home Equity Tracking
Link a property asset to its mortgage loan. The asset card automatically shows your home value, remaining mortgage, and current equity side by side — updates as you make payments.
"How much equity do I have in my house?"
Pro Tips
💡 Add accounts before anything else. Every payment modal pre-selects the default account, overdraft warnings only work when balances are set, and the Monthly Health card compares your balance to what's due.
💡 Use the Type filter in Money to focus. Tap Type in the sort bar to group everything by Income, Bills, Loans, and Credit Cards. Perfect for reviewing just one category at a time.
💡 Mark income Received as it clears. Tap any paycheck card to expand it and hit Received. This keeps your status badges accurate and lets the Monthly Health card reflect reality.
💡 Add a website URL to every bill. Edit a bill and fill in the Website field. When you go to schedule a payment, a Go to Website button opens your biller directly so you can pay and come back to confirm.
💡 Use transfers for savings contributions. Moving money to your emergency fund or HYSA? Use the Transfer tile in Quick Update — it moves the balance correctly and won't inflate your spending stats.
💡 Enter original loan amounts. When adding a car loan or student loan, fill in the Original Loan Amount field. The Debt Planner shows exactly how much progress you have made paying it down.
💡 The Debt Planner sorts 0% promos last on purpose. In Avalanche mode, 0% cards go to the bottom — that's correct. Let them ride free while you attack high-APR debt first.
💡 Undo any delete. Deleted something by accident? A purple undo bar appears for 8 seconds at the bottom of the screen. Tap it to restore.
💡 Export before big changes. Use Settings → Export to Excel to save a backup before loading demo data or resetting the app.
